## 0.1.0-alpha.1 — earlier local candidate

### Added

  six editable parameters, silent start and gentle fallback test tone.
- Versioned parameter restore/save on clean MENU exit with atomic replacement,
  corruption checks and interrupted-save preservation.

### Fixed

- Normal Onion sample launch failed because its audio preload ignored openat's
  directory descriptor. Retained validation using descriptor-anchored procfs paths.
- Avoided unsupported SDL32-bit rotation and redundant unchanged display updates.

### Known limitations

- Early feature subset: no effects, sample browser, recording, MIDI, full project
  compatibility or full GrainBrick parity. Linear interpolation is not band-limited.
- Comprehensive power/sleep and long-duration audio acceptance remain pending.
- Host/ARM builds and dummy drivers do not establish physical audio quality.
